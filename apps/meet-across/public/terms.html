What the service provides

You may use the planner for personal or business scheduling. It compares selected cities, dates, working windows, and meeting durations, and can generate a share link or a calendar file. It does not access participants' calendars, reserve time, send invitations, or guarantee anyone's attendance.

Check a plan before relying on it

Results depend on the values you enter and your browser's time-zone data. Government rule changes and older browser or operating-system data can affect results. Check important meeting times with participants and with your calendar after importing an ICS file. The planner does not identify public holidays, leave, or existing commitments. It does not support work windows that run overnight.

MeetAcross is a planning aid, not an authoritative clock service or a service for emergency, transport, legal-deadline, or other safety-critical timing.

About the project

A small desk for everyday image exports

ImageFit Desk helps people prepare static images for upload forms, documents and web pages. It combines aspect-ratio-preserving resizing with PNG, JPEG and WebP export. For JPEG and WebP, it can try an encoding quality that fits a requested size budget at the dimensions you choose.

The tool is free to use and does not require an account. Selected images are processed in the browser tab, rather than sent to an image-processing server. The site is published by FMFM Stars and hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

A deliberately focused workflow

The tool does not crop, upscale, edit animated images or provide exact archival colour handling. Its limits protect a lightweight browser workflow: inputs up to 10 MiB, at most 8,192 pixels on either axis and at most 16 million pixels in total.

The guide explains how to work with upload limits. The format comparison covers transparency and encoding choices. Keep your original and check every export before relying on it.

Help improve it

Found a problem or a confusing result? Use the contact page to describe the steps and browser you used. A non-sensitive test image is more helpful than a private original.

    Before you press play

    What happens when a negative offset crosses zero?

    A cue that starts before zero but ends later is clipped to a zero start. A cue that ends at or before zero is omitted. You will see the count in a conversion notice. If every cue would disappear, conversion stops with an error.

    Can this fix captions that drift over time?

    This tool applies a constant offset to every cue. It cannot correct a mismatch in video speed or frame rate. If the gap grows as the video plays, use an editor that supports time stretching.

    Does it keep positioning and styling?

    Caption text markup is retained unless you enable its removal. WebVTT positioning settings, notes, regions, style blocks, and cue identifiers are always omitted. Inline WebVTT timing tags used for karaoke-style highlighting are rejected. Conversion is intended for a plain text and timing export.

    Are uploaded subtitles stored?

    The file picker reads text into your browser. Subtitle text is not sent to our server, saved to your account, or used to train a model. You can use the privacy page to learn about normal hosting metadata.

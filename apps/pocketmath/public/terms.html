What the tools provide

You may use PocketMath to calculate a percentage tip, allocate an equal split, or compare one or two successive discounts. No account, subscription, or payment is required. PocketMath does not move money, make purchases, store payment credentials, or connect to financial accounts.

Check the inputs and method

Results depend on the values you enter and the rounding method described in the guides. The tools use nonnegative amounts with two decimal places, round percentage adjustments half up to cents at each stage, and distribute remainder cents across the first shares in an equal split.

They do not perform currency conversion or implement every currency’s denomination rules. They also do not decide tax treatment, whether a tip is appropriate, the eligibility of a coupon, or a merchant’s checkout policy. Check those details against the relevant receipt, agreement, or offer.

Payment and delivery / 支払い・提供時期

Stripe’s hosted checkout accepts the payment methods shown there. The first payment is due at purchase and renewal payments are due monthly until cancelled. Access starts after server-side payment confirmation. A checkout return screen alone does not confirm payment.

Renewal and cancellation / 更新・解約

Use Manage billing / cancel to stop the next renewal. Paid access continues until the end of the current paid period. Cancelling renewal does not itself issue a refund. If the portal is unavailable, contact us for cancellation assistance.

See the billing and usage policy for details.

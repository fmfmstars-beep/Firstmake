How metering works

Bounded usage

Audio duration is rounded up to whole seconds. Text is counted as Unicode code points. Usage is charged to your allowance only after successful processing; failed provider attempts still count toward attempt limits.

Unused allowances do not roll over. There are no automatic overage charges. Processing stops when an allowance or capacity limit is reached.

Results are drafts. Check names, dates, meaning and tone before use. Timing and translation quality vary.

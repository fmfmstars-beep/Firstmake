Privacy and temporary processing.

Updated 5 October 2026 · Current English–Japanese MVP

Fixed examples work without sign-in. Live AI processing and new paid purchases are disabled in the current launch configuration. The processing and retention controls below describe the implementation used when the service is enabled; they are not evidence that a live provider has been validated.

Fixed examples and browser actions

Fixed work examples are prepared English–Japanese pairs shown by the app. Selecting an example does not send its text for AI translation and does not create an account. They are draft examples with human native-speaker review still pending. Copying uses your clipboard; showing a result opens an on-page display. Read aloud uses your browser or operating-system voice service, which may process the text externally. That provider’s policies apply.

Text and recordings sent for processing

When enabled, signed-in cloud translation sends the text and selected language direction to the Worker and the configured AI provider. Optional recording asks for microphone permission and acknowledgement before sending audio. The app accepts recordings of 2–30 seconds and converts them into WAV for transcription, followed by translation.

The application does not persist raw audio in D1 or application object storage. It holds audio in the browser for preview and in request-processing memory. Clear removes the current page’s draft, result and recording. Clearing the browser cannot recall content already sent to a provider.

Cloudflare Workers AI and an optional OpenAI adapter are supported server-side configurations. The deployment determines which provider processes a request. No real-provider accuracy or latency validation has been completed for this launch. Providers may retain technical or submitted data under their own terms; our application retention limit does not promise the same deletion by a provider.

A temporary encrypted result cache

Successful processing stores the original text or audio transcript, translation and timing in an AES-GCM encrypted result record. It supports retrieval of the same request without running AI again. The record has a 15-minute expiry and is accessible only to its authenticated account. After expiry, the app refuses retrieval; scheduled cleanup removes expired rows.

This is temporary storage, not a saved history. There is no permanent Pro text-history feature enabled in this MVP. Cloud infrastructure backups and recovery systems may retain copies beyond an application row’s expiry according to their own retention process. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup.

Accounts, Google and security cookies

New accounts use Google OpenID Connect when configured, with verified identity and email. Account records include an internal ID, Google subject identifier and email, account creation time, access role and any subscription identifiers or status. Earlier beta accounts may retain a hash of a recovery key; do not send that key to support.

A Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite session cookie lasts up to seven days. The database stores its hash rather than the raw cookie token. Sign-in also uses a short-lived challenge cookie and server challenge, plus Cloudflare Turnstile when configured. Google and Turnstile have their own processing policies. Sensitive account and owner operations require recent verified Google authentication. Same-origin checks and session-bound CSRF tokens protect state-changing account requests.

Purchase setup records

A pending subscription purchase keeps its Stripe Checkout identifier, a retry key and the server-created checkout parameters, which may include account email and billing customer identifiers. This prevents repeated payment sessions from creating duplicate subscriptions. Account deletion waits for the pending checkout or resulting subscription to be cancelled; the application then removes this purchase-setup record. Financial records needed for reconciliation remain separate.

Usage and abuse-prevention records

Free usage is limited by account and a salted identity-derived hash. Monthly and daily Free abuse counters are scheduled for removal after 00:00 UTC on the eighth day of the following month, once no active reservations remain. These minimal counters can remain after account deletion to prevent repeated sign-up from resetting the allowance. A hash is pseudonymous data, not a promise of anonymity.

The request ledger stores request IDs, account and input hashes, usage quantities, attempt state, timing, estimated costs and error codes; it does not store plaintext message bodies. Completed or released request records become eligible for cleanup after 90 days. Expired quota and budget records are cleaned up subject to active-reservation and linked-record checks. Rate-limit and authentication counters retain hashes and quantities rather than input text.

Payments and owner records

Paid purchasing is disabled. If enabled later, Stripe handles hosted payment details and billing management. The application can store customer, subscription and verified invoice identifiers plus payment and entitlement records. It does not receive full card numbers. Financial-record retention, seller tax arrangements and refund rules are not yet finalised for sales; required records may remain after an account is deleted.

Server-authorised owners can view customer and retrieved payment metrics after recent Google authentication. Ordinary customers cannot access that dashboard. Access actions can be recorded in an owner audit log. Payment metrics do not establish profit or receipt into a bank account.

Your choices and requests

Use fixed examples without an account. Avoid confidential, regulated or unnecessary personal information. Sign out on shared devices. Account deletion requires recent authentication and may remain pending while cancellation or required record handling is completed. A pending state is not confirmation that all data was deleted. Contact fmfm.stars@gmail.com for access or deletion questions without sending a password or recovery key.

No advertising tracking in this MVP

No advertising tags, third-party analytics scripts or marketing cookies are included. Application observability is configured off and handlers do not intentionally log message or audio bodies; cloud providers can still process technical data under their own policies. No complete-anonymity, absolute-security or compliance guarantee is made.

Provider policies: Cloudflare, Google, OpenAI and Stripe.

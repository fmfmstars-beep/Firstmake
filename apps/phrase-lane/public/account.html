Subscription

Renews monthly until cancelled. Cancel the next renewal through the billing portal; access continues until the current paid period ends.

Pro is USD 9 per month: 7,200 audio seconds, up to 600 audio attempts, 50,000 input text characters and up to 500 text attempts per paid billing period. Unused allowance does not roll over; no automatic overage charge.

Successful source text or audio transcripts and translations are temporarily retained in an encrypted 15-minute result cache. No permanent Pro text history is enabled. Audio is not persisted by the app. See the privacy policy for separate provider, backup and minimal-record retention.

Existing subscribers may still manage billing or cancel when new purchases are disabled, if the payment provider is configured for their account.

Checkout becomes available only when the service and purchase requirements are ready. Review the final amount and renewal terms before paying.

Terms of use.

Updated 5 October 2026 · Current English–Japanese MVP

PhraseLane is an early-stage language utility for adults aged 18 and over. Use it only for lawful purposes and material you are authorised to process. Keep appropriate permission from anyone you record.

Examples and current availability

Fixed work examples are available without sign-in. They are prepared draft pairs, not live AI output, and human native-speaker review is still pending. Arbitrary cloud translation and transcription require an authenticated account and an available service.

Live English–Japanese translation and transcription are unavailable. Fixed examples and the local subtitle tool remain available.

The current app supports English ↔ Japanese messages of 1–1,000 Unicode characters and optional recordings of 2–30 seconds when enabled. The earlier twelve-language interface is an inactive archive. Its old API and network quotas do not describe the current MVP. The local subtitle tool formats drafts in your browser; you must review caption timing yourself.

Free allowances

Free includes 600 audio seconds and up to 60 audio processing attempts per UTC calendar month, with a daily Free audio allowance of 120 seconds. It also includes 5,000 input text characters and up to 50 text translation attempts per UTC calendar month. Daily audio usage resets at 00:00 UTC; monthly allowances reset at the next UTC calendar month.

Audio is rounded up to whole seconds. Text is counted as Unicode code points. Successful processing consumes the relevant usage allowance; failed provider attempts still count toward attempt limits. Duplicate retrieval of the same completed request is not a second AI generation. Service budgets, rate limits or provider outages can stop processing before an individual allowance is exhausted.

Pro subscription and purchase availability

New Pro subscriptions are temporarily unavailable. You can use fixed examples and the local subtitle tool. Existing subscribers can manage billing from their account when configured.

Pro is USD 9 per month and includes 7,200 audio seconds and up to 600 audio attempts, plus 50,000 text characters and up to 500 text attempts per paid billing period. Review the final amount due, billing interval and any applicable tax in checkout before paying. Unused allowances do not roll over and no automatic overage charge is added. Paid access starts after server-side payment confirmation.

The subscription renews monthly until cancelled through the billing portal in your account. Cancellation stops the next renewal; access continues through the current paid period. Contact support if the portal is unavailable. Refund requests are reviewed for duplicate billing, service failures and other billing errors; cancelling renewal does not itself issue a refund. Mandatory consumer rights are unaffected. Read the billing and usage policy and seller information before purchasing.

Review results before using them

Translations and transcripts can contain errors, omit context or change tone. Check names, numbers, dates, commitments and meaning. The service does not provide certified translation, professional legal or medical advice, emergency interpretation or safety-critical instructions. Use qualified human review for consequential work.

No accuracy, availability, response time, commercial result, advertising approval or income outcome is guaranteed. A fluent example or a successful software test is not proof of real-provider quality.

Account security and temporary storage

Google sign-in is available only when configured. Keep any existing beta recovery key private; we store its hash and cannot reveal the original. Sign out on shared devices. State-changing operations use same-origin and CSRF checks, and sensitive changes require recent authentication.

Successful originals or transcripts and translations use an encrypted, 15-minute result cache. Audio is not persisted in application storage. Minimal abuse, request and financial records have separate retention rules, and infrastructure backups and AI providers may have separate retention processes. Account deletion may be pending while cancellation or required record handling is completed. See Privacy for the details.

Fair use and ownership

Do not bypass allowances, attack the service, impersonate others, commit fraud or submit material you have no right to share. Access can be limited for abuse or security reasons. You retain your rights in your input; requesting processing does not transfer ownership to us. Provider services operate under their own terms.

Terms of use

Updated 2 October 2026. PhraseLane is an early-stage language utility for adults aged 18 and over. By using the service, use it only for lawful purposes and material you are authorised to process.

Free beta

The free cloud service provides up to 10 requests per network address per UTC day, with 500 input characters per request. A shared daily capacity limit may temporarily pause cloud translation. Local tools and browser voice capabilities have their own limits. Availability, latency and translation quality are not guaranteed.

Pro subscription availability

Paid subscriptions are not currently offered. The prepared text plan is $9 USD per month with monthly renewal until cancelled, 50,000 input characters per UTC calendar month, up to 1,000 characters per request and 100 cloud attempts per UTC day. The shared beta limit of 200 attempts per UTC day and 6 attempts per minute per network also applies. Failed cloud requests can consume allowances. Monthly character resets are separate from your renewal date. Unused allowances do not roll over. No paid overage is automatically added. Audio-minute packages are not part of this plan. Final price, tax and renewal terms are shown before checkout.

Cancellation through the billing portal stops future renewal; access normally continues until the paid period ends. Refund requests can be submitted through the contact page. Mandatory consumer rights remain applicable, and no blanket exclusion of those rights is intended. A purchase will not be enabled until the operator’s sale disclosures are completed.

Review before relying on output

Machine translation can contain errors or omit context. You are responsible for reviewing names, numbers, tone and meaning. The service does not provide certified translation, medical advice, legal advice, emergency interpretation or safety-critical instructions. Use qualified human review for high-stakes work.

Fair and safe use

Do not bypass rate limits, attack the service, upload material you have no right to share, impersonate others, conduct fraud or record others without appropriate permission. Access can be limited for abuse or security reasons. The service is supplied without a promise of uninterrupted or error-free operation, subject to rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Account security

Keep your recovery key private. It is the credential that restores your account. We store only its hash and cannot show the original again. Sign out on shared devices. Contact the operator about suspected unauthorised access without sending the key itself.

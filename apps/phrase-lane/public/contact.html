A direct line

Questions about the tools, a translation issue or your account? Contact the operator at fmfm.stars@gmail.com.

Tell us what happened

Include the tool name, your browser and a description of the issue. Use a non-sensitive example if a translation behaved unexpectedly. Do not send recovery keys, passwords, payment card details or confidential documents.

Account and billing requests

For data access, deletion or billing questions, describe your request without sharing your private account key. Identity verification may be needed before account changes. No fixed response-time promise is made during this beta.

Report a security issue

Send a brief description and steps that demonstrate the problem without accessing another person’s data. Do not perform disruptive testing. We will investigate reports, but this is not a paid bug-bounty programme.

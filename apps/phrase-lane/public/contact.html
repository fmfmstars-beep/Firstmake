Include the page or tool name, your browser and what happened. Use a short, non-sensitive example if a result behaved unexpectedly. Do not send passwords, account recovery keys, payment card details or confidential documents.

Use Account to sign in, view usage, sign out or request deletion. Sensitive changes require recent verified Google authentication. Describe an access or deletion question by email without sharing your credential; identity verification may be needed before we change records.

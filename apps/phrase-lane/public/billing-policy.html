Billing and usage

Know the limits before you pay.

Updated 5 October 2026

New Pro subscriptions are temporarily unavailable. You can use fixed examples and the local subtitle tool. Existing subscribers can manage billing from their account when configured.

Free and Pro allowances

Free includes 600 audio seconds and up to 60 audio processing attempts per UTC calendar month, plus 5,000 input text characters and up to 50 text translation attempts per UTC calendar month. Free audio has an additional 120-second daily allowance. Each recording must be 2–30 seconds; each text message must be 1–1,000 Unicode characters. Cloud processing requires sign-in and an available service.

Pro is USD 9 per month and includes 7,200 audio seconds and up to 600 audio processing attempts per paid billing period, plus 50,000 input text characters and up to 500 text translation attempts per paid billing period. The 2–30-second limit per recording and 1–1,000-character message limit also apply to Pro.

How usage is counted

Audio duration is rounded up to the next whole second. Input text is counted by Unicode code points rather than bytes. Successful processing consumes the relevant usage allowance. Failed provider processing does not consume audio seconds or input text characters, but the attempt still counts toward the attempt limit.

The app shows any temporarily reserved usage while a request is running. A reservation is not a final charge. Remaining allowance and attempt limits can stop further requests. Service-wide capacity, rate limits or provider outages can also prevent processing.

Renewal and unused allowances

Free monthly limits reset at the start of each UTC calendar month. The daily Free audio allowance resets at 00:00 UTC. Pro limits follow the paid subscription billing period confirmed by the payment provider. Unused allowance does not roll over. No automatic overage charge is added.

Pro renews monthly until cancelled. Payment is handled by Stripe’s hosted checkout. Paid access begins after the server confirms payment; returning from checkout alone does not confirm it. Review the final amount due, currency, billing interval and any applicable tax before paying. Your bank may apply its own currency-conversion fees.

Cancellation

Use Manage billing / cancel in your account to cancel the next renewal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period, and paid access continues until then. Cancelling renewal does not itself issue a refund. If you cannot access the billing portal, contact us for help with cancellation.

Refund requests

Contact fmfm.stars@gmail.com with the account email, payment date and a short description of the issue. We review requests involving duplicate billing, service failures or other billing errors and respond with the outcome. Do not send full payment card details or sign-in secrets. Nothing in this policy limits any mandatory consumer rights.

Account deletion and billing

Deleting an account may require recent sign-in. If cancellation, provider records or legally required records need further handling, the app can report a pending deletion request. Do not assume all records have been deleted while a request is pending.

Questions

Email fmfm.stars@gmail.com for billing questions. Do not send payment card details, passwords or a recovery key.

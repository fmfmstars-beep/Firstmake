Shift earnings

Paid hours exclude unpaid breaks. Gross estimate before taxes and other deductions. Overtime and local wage rules are not calculated.

How rounding works

Tip and wage amounts round to the nearest cent, with half cents rounded up. When a bill cannot divide evenly, the remaining cents go to the displayed number of people so everyone’s share adds up exactly.

For 7 hours 30 minutes, enter 7.50 paid hours. Enter the billed amount after any restaurant taxes or service charges you want included; check whether a tip is already included.

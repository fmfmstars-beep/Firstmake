About this project

This page is the English introduction to Firstmake's KOMOREBI café website concept. The Japanese version contains the complete design walkthrough, sample business information, and implementation notes. Some of that detailed material has not yet been translated.

The website uses illustrated assets and a responsive layout to demonstrate how a café might present its brand online. The example business name, location, hours, prices, and menu are fictional.
